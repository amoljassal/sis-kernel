### Design Principles

1. **Security Foundation**: Cryptographic identity and integrity verification at kernel level
2. **Unified Development**: Hardware and software development are inseparable, synchronized operations
3. **Natural Language First**: Primary interface is conversational, technical interfaces are secondary
4. **Mathematical Correctness**: Formal verification prevents errors in both hardware and software
5. **Real-Time Synchronization**: <1ms latency for cross-domain updates and optimization
6. **Zero-Copy Architecture**: Both hardware designs and software are memory-mapped objects
7. **Deterministic Execution**: All operations reproducible with complete lineage tracing
8. **Distributed by Default**: Every operation scales across global teams and resources
9. **Safety Gradation**: Beginner-safe defaults with professional override capabilities
10. **Backward Compatibility**: All existing cognitive features preserved and enhanced
11. **User Sovereignty**: Complete user control over AI assistance and privacy
12. **Cross-Domain Learning**: System learns from both hardware and software development patterns

2. **Comprehensive Coverage**: All aspects of the problem are addressed
